Calibration Protocol & Safeguards

  • RDAP 404 ≠ Registrable: Register.si RDAP returning 404 indicates only that the object is not currently indexed in the active public database. Registrars may hold lock records, internal reservation lists, or encounter EPP registry rate limits.
  • Gate 6 Requirement: Before any candidate can pass into BUY_CANDIDATE, a human must manually attempt checkout at an accredited registrar and log the verified result.
  • Registry Busy / Could Not Confirm: If the registrar reports a temporary registry timeout or busy state, the gate strictly FAILS, the attempt timestamp is logged, and unlimited subsequent retries are permitted.
  • Zero Automation Rule: Automated headless browser checkout or scraping of registrar search APIs is strictly prohibited to respect terms of service and avoid rate-blocking.
