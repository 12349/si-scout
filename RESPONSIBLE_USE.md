# Responsible Use Guidelines

**SI Scout** is an analytical research workbench and heuristic screening toolkit for the `.si` top-level domain namespace.
It is built strictly for educational, defensive, and academic inquiry. By using this software, you agree to adhere to the following principles.

---

## 1. No Trademark or Brand Squatting
- Do not use this tool to discover, register, or target names that infringe upon registered trademarks, corporate brand identities, product names, or personal names.
- Domain registrations made in bad faith or to capitalize on another party's goodwill violate the [ARNES Alternative Dispute Resolution Scheme (ARDS)](https://www.register.si/postopek-ards/) and international trademark law. Such domains are subject to mandatory forfeiture without compensation.

## 2. No Registrant Harassment or Aggressive Outreach
- Never use data retrieved through RDAP, WHOIS, or drop-tracking to harass, spam, or solicit existing domain owners or registrants.
- All outbound inquiries must be professional, opt-in, compliant with GDPR and ePrivacy regulations, and immediately terminated upon request.

## 3. Rate Limits and Registry Etiquette
- The official Register.si RDAP endpoint is a shared public infrastructure service funded by the academic and research network of Slovenia (ARNES).
- SI Scout is designed to be polite, hardcoding a minimum 1.0-second delay between queries and capping batch executions.
- Always independently check the registry's terms, acceptable use policies, and rate limits before conducting queries against live endpoints.

## 4. Truthful Registrant Contact Data (NIS2 Directive Compliance)
- The `.si` ccTLD operates under Slovenian national regulations aligned with the European NIS2 framework.
- Register.si **prohibits anonymous WHOIS proxy services**. Every `.si` domain registration requires accurate, verifiable contact information.
- Providing inaccurate contact details risks triggering a 21-day verification audit by ARNES, potentially leading to administrative registry suspension of domains under that contact handle.

## 5. Not Financial Advice
- Domain names are speculative, highly illiquid digital assets.
- Screening scores generated by SI Scout reflect structural and lexical heuristics, **not market value or return potential**.
- Historical empirical data demonstrates that domain investment carry costs typically exceed eventual liquidity; expected value is negative. Never risk capital you cannot afford to lose completely.
